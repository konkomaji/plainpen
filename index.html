The open source grammar checker that never trains AI on your writing

Your thoughts are yours. Plainpen checks grammar, spelling, punctuation and style entirely in your browser, so nothing you write is uploaded, stored or used to train an AI model. It is free, with no login and no ads, and it handles long documents.

Your writing is not training data

Many online writing tools process your text on their servers. Plainpen has no server that receives it. The checker runs on your device, in your browser, and the page is built so that it cannot send your text anywhere.

  • Nothing is uploaded

    Your text stays in the page. A strict content security policy blocks any request to another site.

  • No AI inside

    Plainpen uses a dictionary, written rules and simple counting. There is no language model to feed.

  • Open source

    Read every line, run it yourself, or change it. The MIT license lets you keep your own copy.

  • You own what it learns

    Words and choices you teach it are encrypted on your device. Export or erase them any time.

What Plainpen checks

  • Spelling

    A 275,000 word dictionary, ranked by keyboard distance, sound and how common each word is. Proper nouns and your own words are respected.

  • Grammar

    Subject and verb agreement, a and an, repeated words, mixed up pairs like their and there, irregular verbs and more.

  • Punctuation

    Spacing around commas and full stops, repeated marks, and dashes. Plainpen asks you to avoid em dashes and suggests a comma, colon or full stop.

  • Style and tone

    Wordy phrases, passive voice, weak intensifiers and long sentences, tuned to the writing mode you choose.

How it works

  1. Paste your text. It can be a short message or a document with millions of characters.
  2. Choose how you write. Pick a mode and a kind of English. The rules change to match.
  3. Review the notes. Accept, dismiss or teach. Plainpen remembers what is useful to you.

Everything runs in a web worker in your browser. The checker is built from three parts. Skeleton-Rank finds spelling suggestions by sound and keyboard distance. Tag-Lattice matches grammar patterns on word types, not only on exact words. Trust-Gate keeps a trust score for each rule and mode, and quiets rules that you keep dismissing.

Writing modes

Professional
Business email and reports. Flags informal words, weak intensifiers, wordy phrases and exclamation marks.
Technical
Documentation and engineering text. Ignores code, file names and flags, accepts common technical terms, and avoids wording that talks down to the reader.
Simplified (STE)
Follows the ideas of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: short sentences, simple tenses, no modal verbs like should or could, and plain words. Load your own approved word list for full vocabulary checks.
Friendly
Messages to people you know. Contractions and everyday slang are fine.
Casual
Chat and social posts. Understands text speak, stretched words like soooo, and mixed English with Hindi or Bengali words.

Privacy and security

Your text is checked on your device and is never sent to a server. Learned words and settings are stored in your browser and encrypted with AES-GCM using a 256 bit key that the page cannot read. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. The page runs under a strict content security policy and uses no third party code for checking.

Frequently asked questions

Will my writing be used to train AI?

No. Your text never leaves your device, so there is nothing for anyone to collect or train on. Plainpen has no AI model inside it, and the source code is open so you can check.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Checking runs in a web worker inside your browser. Your text is never uploaded. If the site owner turns on optional visit statistics, they only start after you agree, and they never include your text.

Does Plainpen use AI?

No. It uses a dictionary, written grammar rules and simple counting. It does not use a language model.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It is free. There is no login, no ads and no usage limit.

Which kinds of English does it support?

American, British and Indian English spelling, plus an optional Banglish dialect for romanised Bengali words such as Pujo and Pandel. You can add your own names, places and preferred spellings in the Brain.

What is Simplified Technical English?

ASD-STE100 is a controlled language standard for technical writing. Plainpen's Simplified mode checks sentence length, tense, modal verbs and plain word choice. It does not include the official ASD dictionary, which is licensed separately.

How does it learn?

Each rule has a trust score for each writing mode. When you accept or dismiss a suggestion the score changes, and rules you keep dismissing go quiet. You can also teach a word as a name, place, brand, term or preferred spelling, with a note. All of this stays on your device.

How long can the text be?

Up to 12 million characters. Long text is checked in slices so the page stays responsive, and only changed paragraphs are checked again.

Keep Plainpen free

Plainpen is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking of your writing. If it saved you time, you can buy me a coffee. It keeps the side projects alive.
