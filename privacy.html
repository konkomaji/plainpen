Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version: your writing never leaves your device, Plainpen sets no cookies, and nothing you write is collected, sold or used to train AI.

Who we are

Plainpen is an open source grammar and spell checker created and run by Konko Maji. You can contact us at hello@konkomaji.com.

What we do not collect

  • We do not receive, store or read the text you type, paste or open.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name or email address.
  • We do not use your writing to train, test or improve any AI model. Plainpen contains no AI model.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we show no advertising.

What stays on your device

To work as you expect, Plainpen stores a few things in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

  • Your draft, so it is still there when you come back.
  • Your settings, such as writing mode, kind of English and theme.
  • What you teach it: words, names, places, preferred spellings, and which suggestions you accept or dismiss.
  • An offline copy of the app, so it works without a connection.

These items are encrypted with AES-GCM (256 bit) using a key that your browser keeps and that page code cannot read. You can export an encrypted backup, or erase everything, from the Data panel at any time. Clearing your browser data also removes it.

Cookies

Plainpen does not set cookies. If you choose to allow visit statistics (see below), your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie.

Optional visit statistics

The open source project has no analytics. If the site owner turns on Google Analytics for a deployment, a switch appears in the Data panel. It is off until you turn it on. When on, Google receives standard page visit information such as the page address, browser type, approximate location from your IP address (anonymised) and the time. It never receives your writing, because the page sends no custom events. Advertising features are switched off. You can turn it off again at any time, and reloading the page stops all measurement. Google's own privacy policy applies to its handling of that data.

Hosting and server logs

Like any website, the host that serves the files may keep ordinary technical logs, such as the time of a request, the page requested and your IP address, for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you or to track you across sites. They do not contain your writing.

Links to other sites

The page links to services such as Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub, Instagram, LinkedIn and X. If you follow a link, that service's own privacy policy applies. Plainpen does not load their scripts or trackers on this page.

Children

Plainpen collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for general audiences.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You hold the data on your device and control it in the Data panel. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have a privacy question or complaint, write to hello@konkomaji.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will make it clear on the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@konkomaji.com.

Keep Plainpen free

Plainpen is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking of your writing. If it saved you time, you can buy me a coffee. It keeps the side projects alive.
